# User Personas

## Primary Persona 1 – Family Member

### Profile

- Age: 25–60 years
- Relationship: Son, Daughter, Spouse, Parent, or Sibling
- Technical Knowledge: Basic to Intermediate
- Emotional State: Grieving and emotionally stressed

### Goals

- Understand what needs to be done after the loss of a loved one.
- Complete legal and financial formalities correctly.
- Avoid unnecessary office visits.
- Organize all important documents securely.
- Track completed and pending tasks.

### Pain Points

- Doesn't know where to start.
- Government procedures are confusing.
- Different organizations require different documents.
- Fear of missing important deadlines.
- Emotional burden while handling paperwork.

### How Our Platform Helps

- Provides personalized step-by-step guidance.
- Explains every process in simple language.
- Organizes important documents securely.
- Tracks progress throughout the journey.
- Sends gentle reminders for pending tasks.

## Primary Persona 2 – Elderly User

### Profile

- Age: 55+
- Limited digital literacy.
- Comfortable using smartphones but not complex applications.

### Goals

- Understand procedures without legal jargon.
- Receive instructions in Hindi or their preferred language.
- Complete required formalities with confidence.

### Pain Points

- Difficulty understanding English.
- Government websites are difficult to navigate.
- Small text and complex forms.
- Fear of making mistakes.

### How Our Platform Helps

- Multilingual support.
- Large and simple interface.
- Voice-assisted guidance (future roadmap).
- Easy navigation with minimal steps.

---

## Secondary Persona 1 – Common Service Centre (CSC) Operator

### Profile

- Assists citizens with digital government services.
- Handles multiple applications daily.

### Goals

- Reduce repetitive explanations.
- Complete customer requests quickly.
- Maintain proper documentation.

### Pain Points

- Missing documents.
- Customers lack basic knowledge.
- Repeated visits due to incomplete information.

### How Our Platform Helps

- Generates personalized checklists.
- Verifies required documents.
- Keeps all documents organized.

---

## Secondary Persona 2 – Legal Advisor / Lawyer

### Profile

- Provides legal guidance related to succession and documentation.

### Goals

- Receive organized client information.
- Reduce repetitive consultation time.
- Help clients complete processes efficiently.

### Pain Points

- Clients often arrive with incomplete documents.
- Significant time spent explaining basic procedures.

### How Our Platform Helps

- Encourages document organization.
- Provides preliminary guidance.
- Reduces repetitive administrative questions.
